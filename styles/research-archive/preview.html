Keep the character

Usage boundaries

Use

  • Square rules, generous whitespace, and aligned baselines.
  • Specific dates, technologies, locations, and project states.
  • A single terracotta point for an index, arrow, or focus.
  • Separate mobile compositions for dense SVG artwork.

Avoid

  • Glass cards, purple gradients, and decorative shadows.
  • Numbering that does not encode a real order or index.
  • Low-contrast captions or shrinking copy to make it fit.
  • Client names and personal content in reusable tokens.
